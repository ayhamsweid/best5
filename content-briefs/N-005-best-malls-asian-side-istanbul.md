## Search intent

## Best5 selection

1. **Akasya** — best overall and strongest rail/metrobus access
2. **Emaar Square Mall** — best for luxury and destination entertainment
3. **Watergarden İstanbul** — best for dining and an open-air social visit
4. **Hilltown Küçükyalı** — best straightforward M4 option for families
5. **Capitol** — best compact, established option near Üsküdar

## Editorial rules

- Preserve the site concept by listing exactly five primary recommendations.
