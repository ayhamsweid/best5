These are five verification case studies, not a ranking of teaching quality. The fifth case is intentionally a private Turkish school record that publishes an international programme, showing why parents must not confuse MEB legal type with external curriculum accreditation.

## Accuracy rules

- “MEB-approved” means the exact legal institution has Ministry permission and supervision. It does not automatically mean Cambridge, IB, Cognia or NEASC accreditation.
- External accreditation does not replace the MEB institution-opening permit.
- Verify the exact legal name, level and campus. One brand may have separate records for kindergarten, primary, middle and high school.
- Under Law No. 5580, a `Özel Milletlerarası Okul` is a non-higher-education institution attended only by foreign-national students. Confirm eligibility for every passport before paying.
- A private Turkish school may teach an international programme without its MEB type becoming `Özel Milletlerarası Okul`.
- Ask for the `kurum açma izni`, `iş yeri açma ve çalışma ruhsatı` and the Ministry-approved weekly timetable.
- Check any Cambridge, IB, Cognia or NEASC claim separately in the awarding body’s current directory.
- Regulatory information was checked 28 July 2026. This guide is informational, not legal advice.
