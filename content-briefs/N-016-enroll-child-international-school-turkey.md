## Best5 angle

Five practical enrolment stages:

1. Verify the school’s legal type and the child’s eligibility.
2. Build the identity and school-record file.
3. Complete e-Denklik when foreign study must be placed in the Turkish system.
4. Complete the school assessment and obtain a written offer.
5. Review the contract, pay safely and finish onboarding.

This is a five-stage process, not a ranking. It supports families moving from school research to a documented enrolment decision.

## Accuracy rules

- `Özel Milletlerarası Okul` and a private Turkish school offering an international programme are different legal types.
- Under Law No. 5580, an `Özel Milletlerarası Okul` serves foreign-national students. Passport and dual-nationality eligibility must be confirmed before payment.
- Immigration/identity documents differ by status. Do not state that every child needs the same visa or residence document.
- Denklik requirements differ for continuing students and graduates, Turkish and non-Turkish citizens, and document language/country.
- Use the current e-Denklik interface and the Istanbul MEB process guide; take every uploaded original to the appointment.
- Do not promise a processing time. Istanbul MEB says the signature stage may take up to a week, but individual cases vary.
- Never pay a non-refundable amount before the exact legal school, grade placement, total price and refund terms are written.
- Regulatory information checked 28 July 2026. This is informational, not legal or immigration advice.
