## Search intent

Help visitors decide between Istanbul’s Grand Bazaar and Spice Bazaar according to products, scale, time, Sunday availability, location, atmosphere, and itinerary. The article must answer “which one?” rather than re-ranking all Istanbul markets.

## Best5 comparison decisions

1. **Widest range and deepest exploration** — Grand Bazaar
2. **Spices, sweets and edible gifts** — Spice Bazaar
3. **Short visit and simple navigation** — Spice Bazaar
4. **Sunday planning** — Spice Bazaar is usually the practical choice; verify live
5. **Best historic-market day** — visit both when the schedule allows

## Editorial rules

- Preserve the Best5 concept through exactly five decision factors, not five invented markets.
- Do not cannibalise the broad “best traditional markets” article.

- Avoid counterfeit or authenticity guarantees; advise invoices and reputable sellers.
