## Search intent

Help families compare officially authorized IB programmes in Istanbul and distinguish Primary Years Programme (PYP), Middle Years Programme (MYP) and Diploma Programme (DP). Authorization must be checked per programme and campus.

## Best5 editorial ranking

1. **Istanbul International Community School (IICS)** — full PYP–MYP–DP continuum in English and a strongly international admissions context
2. **ENKA Schools Istanbul** — established PYP–MYP–DP continuum integrated with the Turkish national curriculum
3. **Irmak Schools** — bilingual PYP–MYP–DP continuum, with MYP and DP authorization added in 2023
4. **MEF International School Istanbul** — English PYP and long-running DP with Cambridge/IGCSE in the middle years
5. **Eyüboğlu Schools** — long-running MYP and DP at the main school, with PYP/MYP authorization at a separate Kemerburgaz campus

## Ranking method

- Current programme authorization in the official IB directory
- Continuity across PYP, MYP and DP
- Language and curriculum fit
- Campus and admissions transparency
- Honest disclosure of optional, hybrid or split-campus routes

This is an editorial shortlist, not a guarantee of admission, IB Diploma participation, results, university recognition or superiority for every child.

## Editorial rules

- Preserve the Best5 concept with exactly five ranked schools.
- Verify every programme in the official IB directory.
- Do not call a candidate school or candidate programme authorized.
- Do not imply that DP is available to every admitted student.
- State when IB is blended with the Turkish curriculum, Cambridge or IGCSE.
- State when programmes sit at different campuses.
- Explain that the IB Diploma and a school graduation diploma are separate credentials.
- Do not publish volatile fees; request a dated, itemized quote.
- Confirm current university and Turkish equivalency requirements directly.
