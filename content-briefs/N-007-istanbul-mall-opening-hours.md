## Editorial rules

- Preserve the Best5 concept with exactly five verified primary examples.
- State the checked date near every schedule.
- Never claim all Istanbul malls follow the same hours.
- Distinguish mall-building hours from tenant and attraction hours.
- Do not guarantee holiday hours; direct readers to same-day official announcements.
- Avoid fixed last-entry claims because each tenant controls them.
- Refresh before Ramadan Bayramı, Kurban Bayramı, New Year, and any announced special schedule.
