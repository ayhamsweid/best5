# N-012 — International School Fees in Istanbul 2026–2027

## Search intent

- Primary: `international school fees Istanbul`
- Secondary: `international school fees in Istanbul 2026`, `Istanbul international school tuition`, `school fees Istanbul 2026 2027`
- Intent: high-value commercial research. The reader needs a comparable first-year budget, not a generic school ranking.

## Editorial angle

Keep the Best5 format as **five documented fee profiles**, ordered by the lowest published starting annual total, not as a claim about educational quality:

1. Brights International School
2. Istanbul International School
3. MEF International School Istanbul
4. IICS Marmara Campus
5. IICS Hisar Campus

Marmara and Hisar are separate profiles because their published early-years and primary tuition differs. The article must state clearly that they belong to the same school.

## Accuracy rules

- Use only official 2026–2027 schedules checked on 28 July 2026.
- Preserve each school’s published USD currency; do not add a volatile TRY conversion.
- Separate tuition/annual total from application, registration, capital levy, transport, examinations and devices.
- “Starting from” is not a comparable family total until inclusions and compulsory one-time charges are checked.
- Never call the lowest published fee the “best school” or “cheapest final cost”.
- Tell families to obtain a dated written quotation for the child’s grade and campus before paying.

## Key verified figures

- **Brights:** published annual totals are $6,950 preschool, $7,950 primary, $9,000 middle and $9,900 high school. New enrollment is $150 once; seat reservation is included as a $300 component of the annual total. Books/resources/uniform/stationery/trips are grouped under supplies.
- **Istanbul International School:** $21,500 early years; $26,400 grades 1–4; $26,800 grade 5; $27,850 grades 6–7; $28,300 grade 8; $30,200–$32,750 grades 9–12. Tuition, lunch, books/materials, assessment and VAT are stated as included.
- **MEF IS:** annual tuition plus food, VAT included, ranges from $23,610 preschool to $46,265 IB2. New applicants also pay $1,250 application and $4,250 registration.
- **IICS Marmara:** tuition, VAT included, ranges from $23,000 EY3–4 to $48,900 grades 11–12.
- **IICS Hisar:** tuition, VAT included, ranges from $25,200 EY3–4 to $48,900 grades 11–12.
- **IICS one-time charges:** $300 application, $1,200 registration and an $8,000 capital levy for new grade 1+ students. Transport, grades 6–12 MacBooks and specified exams/trips are excluded.
