## Best5 angle

The page compares five currently documented campuses inside Başakşehir district. It is a practical fit ranking, not a universal claim about teaching quality:

1. Ihsan International School — Başakşehir Campus
2. BIS Schools — Bahçeşehir Garden Campus
3. Huda Turkish International School — Kayaşehir
4. Think International Schools — Kayaşehir
5. Alnoor International School — Kayaşehir

The order weighs curriculum clarity, Arabic-family fit, official location evidence, admissions transparency and transport/fee information. Families must verify the exact legal school name, programme authorization and grade availability before paying.

## Accuracy rules

- Do not call a school “accredited” beyond the exact claim or record shown by its official source.
- BIS Bahçeşehir is the BIS Schools national/dual-access division, not automatically the same programme as every British International School campus.
- Huda’s current site advertises American Common Core, British Edexcel and Turkish/YKS routes; confirm which route operates for the student’s grade.
- Think describes a British/American/Islamic blend; this is not presented as a single external qualification.
- Alnoor describes an accredited American curriculum on its own site, but the external accreditor is not named on the page reviewed. Tell readers to request the certificate and scope.
- No school in this comparison publishes a sufficiently clear, dated 2026–2027 campus fee table for like-for-like transcription. Mark fees as “request a dated 2026–2027 quote”.
- Do not publish invented drive times. Tell the reader to test the home-to-campus route during morning and afternoon school traffic.
