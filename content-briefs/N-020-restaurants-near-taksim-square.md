## Search intent

Help a visitor choose a reliable restaurant within a practical walking radius of the Republic Monument in Taksim Square. Cover different needs instead of ranking five similar tourist restaurants:

1. Turkish ocakbaşı
2. Historic Ottoman/Turkish cooking
3. Budget-conscious regional cooking
4. Arabic-family smoked meat
5. Close, family-friendly and late-serving option

## Radius and evidence

- “Near Taksim Square” means roughly 5–15 minutes on foot from the Republic Monument in normal conditions.
- Walking times are planning estimates, not turn-by-turn promises. Crowds, crossings, hills and the chosen exit change them.
- Verify official location, menu and opening hours.
- Use dated menu prices only where a current source is available.
- Explain service charges, weight-priced meat, reservations and tourist-trap checks.
