## Best5 angle

Five documented campuses inside Beylikdüzü district:

1. Learner’s World International School (LWIS) Istanbul
2. Gökkuşağı Schools — Beylikdüzü Campus
3. ZEMAS International College
4. Al Aqsa International Schools
5. Al-Fanar Palestinian International School

This is a practical fit ranking based on current official location evidence, accreditation/curriculum clarity, language support, grade continuity and admissions/fee transparency. It is not a universal ranking of teaching quality.

## Accuracy rules

- LWIS currently states NEASC accreditation and Turkish Ministry licensing. Older school information called it an IB PYP candidate; do not call it an IB World School without a current IB record.
- Gökkuşağı’s campus page states Cambridge code TR072 and English-medium teaching. Confirm which Cambridge/IB route applies to the child’s campus and grade.
- ZEMAS states Turkish Ministry licensing, five-year Cognia accreditation and Common Core. Because “five-year” accreditation can expire, request the current certificate and scope.
- Al Aqsa describes separate Yemeni/Arabic and American/English divisions. Do not merge them into a single diploma.
- Al-Fanar follows a developed Palestinian curriculum with Arabic plus English and Turkish; do not label it American or British.
- The only itemized official fee table found for LWIS is for 2024–2025. It may be used to show fee components but not as a current 2026–2027 price.
- Request dated 2026–2027 quotations from all five schools and separate tuition from registration, books, uniform, technology, snacks/meals, examinations and transport.
