## Best5 angle

Five published Islamic-environment options:

1. İhsan International School
2. ZEMAS International College
3. Huda International Schools
4. Think International Schools
5. Alnoor International School

The ranking weighs published Quran/Islamic Studies and Arabic provision, academic-path clarity, current legal traceability, grade continuity and admissions transparency. It is not a theological endorsement or a universal ranking of teaching quality.

## Accuracy rules

- “Islamic” describes the school’s published environment, values or subjects; it is not an MEB school type or external accreditation.
- Verify MEB licence, campus and level separately from Islamic programme claims and external curriculum accreditation.
- Ask for the current grade-by-grade weekly timetable: Quran, tajwid/memorisation targets, Islamic Studies, Arabic, Turkish and core-subject language.
- Ask about religious approach, prayer, dress, food, celebrations, music/arts, gender arrangements, pastoral care, safeguarding and inclusion. Do not infer them from the school name.
- İhsan publishes the clearest English-core plus Arabic, Turkish, Islamic Studies and Quran mix. Match the child to the exact İhsan legal division/campus.
- ZEMAS publishes Islamic culture and strong Arabic as the language of Islam and the Quran; verify current external accreditation scope separately.
- Huda publishes Arabic/Islamic education, character programmes, mosque and multi-route curricula. The current MEB directory was not directly matched under the marketing name during this review; require the exact current Turkish legal name and record before payment.
- Think publishes a British/American/Islamic blend. This is the school’s integrated model, not proof of a particular external diploma.
- Alnoor publishes an American curriculum and a mission inspired by Islamic values. Its current MEB records cover international primary, middle and high school; external accreditor details remain thin publicly.
- Information checked 28 July 2026. Request a dated 2026–2027 offer and current documents.
