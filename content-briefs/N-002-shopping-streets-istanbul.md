### Best5 selection

1. İstiklal Avenue — best overall and best for a first visit
2. Abdi İpekçi Street — best for luxury labels and Turkish designers
3. Bağdat Avenue — best long-form Asian-side shopping day
4. Bahariye Street — best local Kadıköy experience
5. Serdar-ı Ekrem and Galata — best for smaller designer boutiques

### Editorial rules

- Do not turn bazaars or malls into extra ranked entries.
