## Search intent

Help families distinguish an American high-school diploma from Common Core alignment, AP courses, an IB pathway, Turkish-curriculum schools with “American” in their name, and a school’s own accreditation claims.

## Best5 editorial ranking

1. **Istanbul International Community School (IICS)** — strongest externally documented U.S. diploma route, with CIS/NEASC accreditation and IB/AP options
2. **Istanbul American Schools (IAS)** — most direct published K–12 American model and American Diploma Programme
3. **Ihsan International School** — American-curriculum option with Cognia status claimed by the school and several European-side campuses
4. **Elite Academy** — K–12 American-curriculum and STEM option with Cognia status claimed by the school
5. **Brights International School of Istanbul** — published Common Core-aligned K–12 option with MEB and AIAASC status claimed by the school

## Ranking method

- Diploma and curriculum clarity
- Independent accreditation evidence where available
- Continuity across grades
- Admissions and campus transparency
- Honest disclosure of eligibility and unverified claims

The ranking is editorial, not a guarantee of quality, admission, accreditation, diploma equivalency or university acceptance.

## Editorial rules

- Preserve the Best5 concept with exactly five ranked schools.
- Do not treat Common Core alignment, AP participation and accreditation as the same thing.
- Do not imply that the U.S. Department of Education directly accredits an overseas K–12 school.
- Attribute Cognia/AIAASC claims to the school unless independently verified in the accreditor’s current directory.
- State that IICS is IB-led in earlier grades and restricts admission under local rules; it is not a conventional Common Core school.
- Do not include Robert College or Üsküdar American Academy as American-curriculum international schools merely because of their names.
- Do not publish volatile fees; request a dated, itemized quote.
- Tell families to verify diploma equivalency with the relevant Turkish authority for their circumstances.
