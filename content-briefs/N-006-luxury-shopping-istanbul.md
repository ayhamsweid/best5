## Search intent

Help visitors compare Istanbul’s most useful luxury-shopping destinations instead of presenting a generic list of expensive malls. Cover both designer streets and enclosed malls, both European and Asian sides, international houses and Turkish designers, plus practical access.

## Best5 selection

1. **İstinyePark** — best overall luxury mall
2. **Nişantaşı & Abdi İpekçi Street** — best designer-street experience
3. **Zorlu Center** — best combination of luxury brands and direct transit
4. **Bağdat Caddesi** — best Asian-side luxury street
5. **Emaar Square Mall** — best Asian-side luxury destination mall

## Editorial rules

- Preserve the site concept with exactly five primary recommendations.
- Treat the order as an editorial Best5 ranking, not a permanent or objective league table.
- Do not guarantee a particular brand remains open; tell readers to verify current directories.
- Do not promise discounts, VAT refunds, stock, appointment availability, or fixed journey times.
- Advise readers to buy from official boutiques or authorised retailers and retain invoices.
- Keep outlet/bargain intent separate and link to the outlet guide instead.
