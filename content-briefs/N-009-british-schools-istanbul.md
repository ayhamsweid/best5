## Search intent

Help families compare five Istanbul schools that offer a clearly published British or Cambridge pathway. The article must distinguish a full British-school model from Cambridge-to-A-Level and Cambridge-to-IB hybrid routes instead of presenting them as interchangeable.

## Best5 editorial ranking

1. **British International School Istanbul** — strongest full British pathway and externally verifiable accreditation profile
2. **Tarabya British Schools** — Cambridge and A-Level route alongside a Turkish diploma
3. **Istanbul International School** — continuous Cambridge route through IGCSE and AS/A Levels
4. **MEF International School Istanbul** — Cambridge-to-IB hybrid for families who want IGCSE followed by IB DP
5. **Ihsan International School** — multilingual Istanbul option whose published British route is concentrated at high-school level

## Ranking method

- Curriculum clarity and continuity
- External authorization or accreditation that can be checked
- Fit for the keyword’s British-curriculum intent
- Published campus and admissions information
- Honest disclosure of hybrid or dual-programme differences

This is an editorial shortlist, not a claim that one school is universally best. Families must confirm the current programme, authorization, eligibility, placement, fees, refunds, transport, learning support and safeguarding directly before paying.

## Editorial rules

- Preserve the Best5 concept with exactly five schools and ranks 1–5.
- Do not describe Cognia as British accreditation.
- Describe MEF as a Cambridge-to-IB hybrid, not a full British curriculum.
- Describe Tarabya as a dual Cambridge/Turkish pathway.
- Describe Ihsan’s British A-Level pathway as high-school-specific unless the school publishes otherwise.
- Do not publish volatile fee figures; request a dated, itemized quote.
- Do not promise admission, diploma recognition or university acceptance.
- Tell families to verify authorizations in the awarding body’s directory and with the school.
- Use the existing generic international-schools image where a verified school-specific image is unavailable.
