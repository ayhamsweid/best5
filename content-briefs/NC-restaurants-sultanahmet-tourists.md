## Best5 search intent

Exactly five useful, different meal experiences around Sultanahmet:

1. Tarihi Sultanahmet Köftecisi Selim Usta — classic fast Turkish meal
2. Matbah Ottoman Palace Cuisine — Ottoman palace cooking
3. Balıkçı Sabahattin — seafood in a historic house
4. Şehzade Cağ Kebap — regional kebab near Sirkeci
5. Işıl Garden — garden/terrace and long hours

The article must help a tourist choose by experience, walking time, menu evidence,
price planning and reservation need. It must not claim that every venue is cheap or
that a view guarantees food quality.

## Evidence rules

- Walking times are estimates from the Sultanahmet/Blue Mosque area.
- Verify the exact venue and current priced menu before ordering.
- State whether fish is priced by item, 100 grams or kilogram.
- Check bread, water, appetisers and service charges.
- Menu snapshots are dated and can change.
- Şehzade is in nearby Sirkeci rather than inside Sultanahmet; disclose the longer walk.
