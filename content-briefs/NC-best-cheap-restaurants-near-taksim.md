## Evidence and price policy

- “Near” means roughly 1–18 minutes on foot from the Republic Monument.
- Walking times are planning estimates; crowds, hills and the chosen route change them.
- “Cheap” is relative to the Taksim tourist district, not a claim that every menu item is inexpensive.
- Prefer a complete low-cost order at each venue and warn about extras.
- State the evidence date. Menu prices can change quickly and delivery prices can differ.
- Kızılkayalar has a branch-specific official menu dated 5 December 2025 with VAT included.
- Nizam publishes its official Taksim branch and product menu, but not dependable public prices.
- Current directory records support the remaining location, hours and price-band checks.
